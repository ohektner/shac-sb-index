| 2026-11-12 | **Task 1.9 calibration, the open details** (full spec: `docs/CALIBRATION.md`, which is part of this entry; made before any number against a line was computed). The curve is `cover_prob = 1 / (1 + exp(−b × abs(edge)))`: edge in points against the scored line, one slope `b` per predictor per scored season, **no intercept**, no cap, fit by plain maximum likelihood with no penalty. Fit seasons for season S are the eligible seasons before S, with leave-one-season-out margins (the baseline gets its own leave-one-out home court). Pushes, zero-edge games and games with no close are out of the fit and counted. A zero or negative `b` is kept as it is: no calls, and the scorecard says so. Cutoffs include their lower edge (exactly 56% is a Pick).
