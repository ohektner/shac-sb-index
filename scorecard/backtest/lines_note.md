**About the lines.** Lines come from CBBD and are not verified closing lines. Where we could check them against our own pre-tip snapshots (Draft Kings, 2026-27, 209 games), CBBD's line was within half a point of the close in 88% of games (185 of 209), and nearer the close than the open in 91% of the 155 games where the line moved. Earlier seasons can't be checked directly. Results are scored against CBBD's line, which may be slightly earlier than a close. Both the model and Torvik face the same line.
